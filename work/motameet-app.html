MotaMeet — meet people who love what you love. (2026)

MotaMeet matches people through shared passions, not just photos. Everyone picks their motas, and the app finds the people, stories and groups that share them.

01

The story

Most apps start with a photo. MotaMeet starts with what you love. A mota is an icon for an interest, and two people who share motas already have something to talk about.

02

How it works

One loop runs the whole app. Every new mota brings new people, stories and groups.

  1. Pick motasAnything you love, from dogs to road trips
  2. Get matchedPeople who share at least one mota
  3. Explore storiesPhotos and a line of text for each mota
  4. Join groupsCrowds built around shared motas
  5. Chat & meet upOne to one or with the whole group
New motas, new people
03

Product architecture

Before any screen was drawn, the whole product was mapped: one entry point with onboarding questions, branching into four main sections. Pink marks main sections, orange tabs, gray content, green and red positive and destructive actions.

04

Getting set up

A few questions with Moti, then a five-field profile. The first matches appear before the app ever feels like a form.

  1. 1Meet MotiWhat is a mota?
  2. 2Why are you here?HobbiesBusinessRelationship
  3. 3How do you meet?In personOnline chats
  4. 4Pick motas
  5. 5Create profilePhotoNameBirthdayGenderLocation
  6. 6First matches347people share a mota

  • A guide, not a form

    Moti asks every question in a speech bubble and reacts to each answer.

  • Three reasons to join

    Hobbies, business or relationship. One tap sets what the app shows first.

  • Search any mota

    Type “dog” and get Dog walking, Labrador or Husky. Picked motas collect at the bottom.

05

Matching

Matches are made by motas, not by looks. Matthew shares three motas with Jonathan and three with Samantha, and Camping links all three of them.

347 matches

  • Matched by

    Every card opens with the motas you share. Tap one to see that person’s photos for it.

  • Tagged

    Star a person to keep them in a short list next to All.

  • Filters

    Narrow matches by your motas, distance, age range and gender.

  • About

    Gender, age and city on a small map, below the motas, never above them.

06

Mota stories

Each mota can carry photos and a short story, so a profile reads like a set of small moments.

  • Story per mota

    Photos and a caption for every mota on a profile.

  • Likes

    A quick way to say “me too” before the first message.

  • How others see me

    Preview your own profile exactly as a match will see it.

07

Groups & chat

From a group list to a weekend plan in three screens.

Pine Lake, Saturday

  • Based on my motas

    Groups are suggested by your motas, with member count and shared motas on each card.

  • Discover, Joined, Created

    Three tabs keep new groups apart from the ones you are in or run.

  • Group page

    Details, members and stories from the last meetup.

  • Group chat

    Every member in one thread, with names in colour so it stays readable.

08

Messages & profile

One inbox for people and groups, and one profile built from motas.

  • All, Group, Direct

    Filter the inbox in one tap. Unread chats get a pink dot.

  • Search messages

    Find a plan or a person across every conversation.

  • Edit my motas

    Add or remove motas and attach photos to each one.
