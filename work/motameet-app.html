MotaMeet — meet people who love what you love. (2026)

MotaMeet matches people through shared passions, not just photos. Everyone picks their motas, and the app finds the people, stories and groups that share them.

01

The story

Most apps start with a photo and leave you to find something to talk about. MotaMeet starts from the other end: what you actually love.

A mota is an icon that describes an interest, and it can be anything: dogs, cars, concerts, hiking, wine. Two people who share motas already have a reason to talk, so the first message is easy and the first plan is close.

02

Use cases

During onboarding people choose why they are here and whether they want to meet in person or online. That one choice covers very different needs.

  1. Hobbies

    Find someone to hike, paint or go to concerts with, nearby or online.

  2. Business

    Connect with people who work in the same field or care about the same craft.

  3. Relationship

    Start from common ground instead of a photo and a guess.

  4. Finding a crowd

    Join a group built around one mota, share stories and plan get-togethers.

03

Meet Moti

Moti is the guide through the app. He explains what a mota is, reacts to every choice in onboarding and keeps the empty moments friendly.

This is the real thing, not a video: the same Rive file and triggers the iOS and Android apps use, running live in your browser. Try him out.

04

Product architecture

Before any screen was drawn, the whole product was mapped: one entry point with onboarding questions, branching into four main sections. Pink marks main sections, orange tabs, gray content, green and red positive and destructive actions.

05

Onboarding

Moti explains what a mota is, asks what you are looking for and lets you search for anything you love. No long form before the app gets interesting.

06

Matching

Right away you see how many people share at least one mota with you. Each card leads with what you have in common, and mota stories show the person behind the interest: long rides, weekend road trips, the best pizza in town.

07

Groups & chat

Groups gather people around motas. You can browse the ones that match you, read what members share, and jump into the conversation.

08

Messages & profile

All conversations live in one list. On your own profile you add photos and a line of text to each mota, and can check how others see you.

09

On the web

The website explains the idea in six simple steps, with Moti welcoming visitors and a QR code to get the app.

10

In the stores

The store images tell the same story in a few seconds each: shared passions, motas, stories, groups, plans. Swipe or use the arrows.
