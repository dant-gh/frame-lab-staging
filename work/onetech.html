OneTech — a bold monogram with a red corner. (2020)

The OneTech mark is a heavy, geometric monogram built on a grid, with a single red rounded corner as its signature. The same corner becomes a graphic element across the brand.

Client
OneTech
Year
2020
Scope
Logolockupscolourstypography
Typeface
Raleway Light & ExtraBold
01

Construction

The letters are drawn on a grid with circles setting every curve, then filled in as solid shapes.

02

Colour

A strong red for the accent, a deep blue-black for the letters and a cool grey for the lettering.

Primary#EF0C0C · Pantone 185 C
Secondary#242930 · Pantone 433 C
Lettering#7B8287 · Pantone 430 C
03

Lockups & safe space

The mark works alone, above the name or beside it. Spacing and alignment are defined for each position.

04

Sizes & backgrounds

Minimum sizes are 180, 120 and 60 px; below 60 px (21 mm) the lettering is dropped. The logo works on white, dark and red backgrounds.

05

Graphic element & type

The red rounded corner becomes a brand element: its height is 20% of the format’s shorter side, placed in the bottom-left corner. Raleway Light and ExtraBold set the typography.
