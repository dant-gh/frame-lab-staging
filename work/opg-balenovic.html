OPG Balenović — honey from Velebit pastures. (2026)

OPG Balenović is a family farm making honey — “med s velebitskih pašnjaka”. The mark is drawn over the Croatian coat of arms: the chequered shield becomes the stripes of a beehive, the crown becomes mountain peaks, and a drop of honey falls below.

Client
OPG Balenović
Year
2026
Scope
Logoconstructionlabel design
Industry
Honey production
01

Concept

Starting from the Croatian coat of arms anchors the brand in place and tradition. The shield’s rows simplify into hive stripes, the crown’s points into peaks, and a honey drop finishes the shape.

02

Construction

Every part follows the grid, with a small set of corner radii (1, 3 and 5 units) keeping the stripes, peaks and drop consistent.

03

Logo

A monochrome mark, a colour version with grey-blue peaks and a yellow honey drop, and a warm brown variant. Lockups set the name in a classic serif, with or without the tagline.

04

Colour

Honey orange carries the brand, with a mountain grey-blue and a bright drop of yellow. A warm brown is used for the name.

Label

The jar label puts the logo and product name first — “Med Bagrem”, acacia honey — with space for the farm’s story about tradition and Velebit pastures.
