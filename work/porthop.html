Porthop — book your berth now, come whenever. (2015)

A berth booking app for iOS and Android that lets sailors on the Croatian coast find a port, marina or buoy, pick their dates and pay in a few taps.

Client
Porthop
Year
2015
Platform
iOS & Android
Tools
SketchIllustratorInVisionAfter Effects
01

About

Book now, come whenever. Enjoying the Croatian sea and sun is now possible every day until sunset. Book your berth with Porthop and stop worrying about being late.

How it works

Booking takes five steps: find a place, view the location details, book a date, make a payment and check upcoming trips.

02

User experience

Simplicity, speed and clear information were the focus while building Porthop. People don’t need to register straight away, so they can explore the product first.

When they need to book a berth quickly, they can register or sign in within the flow and carry on uninterrupted.

Sitemap & app structure

The app is built around a map home screen with three main sections: Trips, Search and Profile. Booking a trip starts from a location found on the map or through search.

03

UI design

There are three main docking types — port, marina and buoy — so colour coding follows the written content and labels to tell them apart while moving through the app’s sections and content.

Icons

Simple, clean icons back up the content visually, making it quicker to understand and tell apart ports, amenities, actions and more.

Illustrations

Onboarding screens use simple illustrations to make the process a bit more interesting and fun. Colourful graphics match the relaxed, positive mood.

Layout

Colour coding, white space and icons were combined into repeatable modules using an atomic design approach, so every screen is built block by block. Predefined elements keep the content consistent.

04

Animation & interaction

Improving the experience with smooth transitions. Animated transitions connect the flows and help people understand where they are in the app.

They also make the whole experience more interesting: people get more feedback on their actions and feel more secure using the product.
